# Review actions, not intent

Intent — a story, epic, or document stating what is wanted — is created freely
on any surface and is never blocked, reviewed, or rejected at creation. An
intent-only story (no plan, no embedded workflow) is well-formed: the executor
authors its refinement at pickup, and the workflow's first gate judges that
refinement and every action after it. Never demand refinement at creation;
never advance work past a gate because the intent "looked done".

Why: judgment placed at creation blocks the people expressing the need and
reviews nothing real; judgment placed at the gates reviews the executor's
actual actions. One rule forbids both failure modes — gatekeeping intent, and
rubber-stamping action.

See [[reviewer-only-model]] for the roles that enforce the gates, and the
surface contract for where each write lands.
