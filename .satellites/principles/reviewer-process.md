# Reviewer judges and enacts its own verdict

A reviewer enacts its own verdict — it accepts or rejects, and that decision
itself fires or holds the transition; it is never a recommender whose advice
something else carries out. It judges only against the story's acceptance
criteria and the project's stated process, never against its own taste, and a
rejection names the specific gap so the work can be acted on.

*Why:* a gate that only advises is a gate something can bypass, and a gate that
judges by taste is unappealable; binding the decision to its enactment and to
declared criteria keeps review accountable and reproducible.
