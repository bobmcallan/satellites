# satellites · project intent

The worked example of an **intent Document** (see [[substrate-taxonomy]]): the
project's why and its definition of "good", consulted to inform judgement. It
gates nothing and prescribes no step.

## Why satellites exists

Every tool before satellites baked the story lifecycle into the binary — one
team's process shipped as law. satellites ships the **mechanism** and lets each
project state its own process as configuration: skills for the process,
principles for the constraints, reviewers for the gates. The satellites project
is the worked example — prove the mechanism by dogfooding it here before asking
any other project to adopt it.

## What "good" means here

- **Configuration over code.** A behaviour that can be data is data — a skill, a
  principle, a document — not a Go branch.
- **The reviewer-only model holds.** The agent executes; satellites reviews and
  enacts; status is the truth. Nothing is `done` without the reviewer's accept.
- **Smallest change that serves the objective.** Prefer an existing verb, tag,
  or document over a new abstraction.

This document is intent, not instruction: read it to understand what the project
is reaching for; obey the principles and follow the skills for what to do.
